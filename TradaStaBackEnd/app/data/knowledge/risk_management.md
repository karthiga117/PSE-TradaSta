# Risk Management

## Position sizing

Position sizing determines how much capital to allocate to a trade. A common rule is to risk only a small percentage of account equity per trade.

## Risk and reward

Risk/reward compares the potential loss to the potential gain. A trade with a 1:2 risk/reward ratio means the expected gain is twice the risk.

## Drawdown controls

Drawdown limits prevent large portfolio declines. Traders often cap daily loss and overall exposure to preserve capital during adverse market conditions.
