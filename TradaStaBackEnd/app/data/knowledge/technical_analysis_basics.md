# Technical Analysis Basics

## Trend analysis

Trend analysis helps traders identify the dominant direction of price movement. In a bullish trend, prices tend to make higher highs and higher lows. In a bearish trend, prices tend to make lower highs and lower lows.

## Support and resistance

Support is a price level where buying interest can appear. Resistance is a price level where selling interest can appear. These zones are useful for planning entries and exits.

## Market context

Technical analysis works best when combined with market context, volume, and risk management. It is not a guarantee of future price movement.
