# Relative Strength Index (RSI)

## RSI concept

The Relative Strength Index measures the speed and change of price movements. It is typically calculated over 14 periods and oscillates between 0 and 100.

## Overbought and oversold

An RSI value above 70 is often considered overbought. An RSI value below 30 is often considered oversold. Traders use these levels to assess possible turning points.

## Interpretation

RSI can confirm a trend, warn of exhaustion, or highlight divergence. A weak trend with a high RSI may indicate loss of momentum.
