# Trading Strategy Overview

## Moving average trend

A moving average trend strategy compares short-term and long-term averages. When the short-term average sits above the long-term average, the market often appears bullish.

## Confirmation

Strategy confirmation can come from trend direction, momentum, and risk checks. These tools reduce false positives and keep decisions explainable.

## Deterministic decisioning

TradaSta uses deterministic rule-based evaluation for strategies. The system does not allow an LLM to override risk limits or trade execution constraints.
